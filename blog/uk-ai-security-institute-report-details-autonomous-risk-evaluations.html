UK AI Security Institute Report Details Autonomous Risk Evaluations

AI Safety, AISI, Cybersecurity, Autonomous Agents, Governance · 2026-08-20

Security evaluations by the UK AI Security Institute highlight autonomous decision-making observed during routine system testing. The findings underscore an urgent need for standardized guardrails and real-time intervention frameworks as multi-step agentic systems gain broader access.
