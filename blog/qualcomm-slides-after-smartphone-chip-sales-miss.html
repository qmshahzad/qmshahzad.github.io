Qualcomm Slides After Smartphone Chip Sales Miss

Qualcomm, smartphone chips, semiconductor earnings, Apple supply chain, mobile chips, QCOM stock · 2026-11-06

Qualcomm shares fell after its fourth-quarter smartphone chip sales missed Wall Street estimates, with the company citing sluggish demand and lower Apple-related revenue. The result added pressure to an already uneasy semiconductor market and underscored continued softness in parts of the smartphone supply chain. Investors will watch whether premium AI-phone demand can offset weaker mainstream shipments. #Qualcomm #Smartphones #Semiconductors
