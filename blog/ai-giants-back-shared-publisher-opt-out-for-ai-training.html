AI Giants Back Shared Publisher Opt-Out for AI Training

AI training, robots.txt, publishers, OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, Anthropic, content licensing, web crawling · 2026-11-07

OpenAI, Google, Microsoft and Anthropic have pledged support for a shared robots.txt signal that lets publishers block AI training across participating companies with one rule. Anthropic has begun rolling it out, OpenAI plans implementation in coming months, and Google and Microsoft have indicated support beginning in 2027. The proposal could simplify content controls for publishers while leaving search visibility unaffected. #AI #Publishing #robots.txt
