SpaceX Catches Starship From Orbit

SpaceX, Starship, Starbase, reusable rocket, Starlink, aerospace, launch tower · 2026-11-15

SpaceX’s Starship returned from a 22-hour orbital mission and was caught by the launch tower’s mechanical arms at Starbase, Texas. The upper stage deployed 26 Starlink satellites and completed 14 orbits. The Super Heavy booster was not recovered, leaving full two-stage reusability as the next milestone. #SpaceX #Starship #SpaceTechnology
