Apple’s First Touchscreen MacBook Pro Arrives Today

Apple, MacBook Pro, touchscreen Mac, OLED iPad mini, M6 iMac, A20 Pro, product launch · 2026-11-06

Apple is releasing five products today: an OLED iPad mini with A20 Pro, an M6 iMac, a 14-inch M6 MacBook Pro, and 14- and 16-inch MacBook Pro models with OLED touchscreens. The touchscreen MacBook Pros are Apple’s first touchscreen Macs, with pre-orders already open and launch-day availability beginning today. #Apple #MacBookPro #iPadMini
