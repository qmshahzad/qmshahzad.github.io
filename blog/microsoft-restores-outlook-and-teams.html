Microsoft Restores Outlook and Teams

Microsoft, Outlook, Teams, outage, cloud services, Microsoft 365, Copilot · 2026-11-16

Microsoft says it has restored service after a global outage that disrupted Outlook and Teams for nearly two days. The incident underscores the operational risk of concentrated cloud collaboration platforms. It also arrives as Microsoft changes Microsoft 365 Copilot resale arrangements, with add-on license sales ending December 1. #Microsoft #Cloud #Outage
