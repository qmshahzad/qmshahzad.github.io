TSMC Reportedly Weighs 3D DRAM Production

TSMC, 3D DRAM, memory, Nvidia, semiconductor, AI hardware · 2026-11-09

TSMC is considering expanding into advanced memory chips, including 3D DRAM, The Information reported via Reuters. The move reportedly follows a request from Nvidia, its largest customer. The report comes amid an AI-driven memory shortage that has pushed up prices and strained supply. #TSMC #NVIDIA #MemoryChips
