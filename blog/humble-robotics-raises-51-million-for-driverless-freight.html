Humble Robotics Raises $51 Million for Driverless Freight

Humble Robotics, autonomous freight, electric trucks, robotics funding, Series A, logistics, autonomous vehicles · 2026-11-07

Humble Robotics has raised a $51 million Series A led by Obvious Ventures, bringing its total funding to $75 million. The San Francisco startup builds autonomous, electric freight vehicles designed to move goods between ports, rail terminals, warehouses and distribution centers. The funding will support customer pilots, testing, early manufacturing and expansion of its modular vehicle platform. #Robotics #AutonomousVehicles #Freight
