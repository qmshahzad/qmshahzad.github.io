Alphabet Takes $548 Million Stake in Qualcomm

Alphabet, Google, Qualcomm, AI chips, data center, investment · 2026-11-10

Alphabet has acquired a $548 million stake in Qualcomm, Reuters reports. The investment comes as Qualcomm pushes further into custom AI processors for cloud providers seeking alternatives to Nvidia’s costly AI chips. It signals growing competition across the AI data-center silicon market. #Alphabet #Qualcomm #AIChips
