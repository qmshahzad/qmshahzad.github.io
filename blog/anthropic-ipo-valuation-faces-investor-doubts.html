Anthropic IPO Valuation Faces Investor Doubts

Anthropic, IPO, valuation, AI market, investment, roadshow · 2026-11-13

Potential investors in Anthropic’s anticipated IPO are raising doubts about whether the company can proceed with its planned roadshow next week in time for a November listing, Reuters reported.
