OpenAI Adds Wallet for AI Agents

OpenAI, ChatGPT Wallet, AI agents, payments, fintech, ChatGPT Pro, digital payments · 2026-11-16

OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet, allowing eligible U.S. ChatGPT Pro subscribers to authorize AI agents to make purchases. Built with American Express, Mastercard and Visa, it uses payment tokens and requires biometric approval for each transaction. The feature signals a shift toward commerce performed by AI agents. #OpenAI #ChatGPT #AIAgents
