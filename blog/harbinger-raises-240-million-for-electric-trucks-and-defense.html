Harbinger Raises $240 Million for Electric Trucks and Defense

Harbinger, electric trucks, Series D, defense technology, battery storage, autonomous vehicles, startup funding · 2026-11-08

Electric-truck startup Harbinger has raised a $240 million Series D round co-led by Tiger Global, Capricorn’s technology impact fund and Ridgeline. The company says the funding will support an expanded product portfolio, including battery storage and defense applications. Harbinger has raised more than $650 million to date and has been developing hybrid and driverless platforms for commercial and military use. #Harbinger #ElectricVehicles #DefenseTech
