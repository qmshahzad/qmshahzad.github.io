Google Expands Its AI Chip Lineup

Google, TPU, TPU 8i Max, TPU 8tx, AI chips, cloud computing, AI infrastructure · 2026-11-16

Google announced the TPU 8i Max and TPU 8tx, expanding its eighth-generation AI accelerator portfolio. The 8i Max is designed for memory-intensive workloads, while the 8tx emphasizes security. Expected in 2027, the systems could offer enterprises more specialized alternatives to Nvidia-based AI infrastructure. #Google #TPU #AIInfrastructure
