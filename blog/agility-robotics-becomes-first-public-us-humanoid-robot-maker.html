Agility Robotics Becomes First Public US Humanoid Robot Maker

Agility Robotics, Digit 5, humanoid robots, Nasdaq, SPAC, robotics IPO, warehouse automation · 2026-11-08

Agility Robotics debuted on the Nasdaq after a SPAC merger, with related financings raising $465 million. The company says the capital will help commercialize Digit 5, add manufacturing capacity and support more than $300 million in pre-orders. Agility plans an early-access program for Digit 5 in the first half of 2027, marking a milestone for humanoid robotics in public markets. #Robotics #HumanoidRobots #IPO
