Gemini 3.8 Live Adds Real-Time AI Avatars

Gemini 3.8 Live, Live Avatar, Google AI, multimodal AI, voice agents, enterprise AI, SynthID · 2026-10-02

Google introduced Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar on September 24, adding near-real-time video generation to live conversational AI. Google says the enterprise feature can listen, see and speak while using asynchronous tools in the background, and it supports multilingual speech-to-speech interaction. The company also says generated audio and video are watermarked with SynthID. For developers, the release expands the design space for interactive voice-and-visual agents. #Gemini #GoogleAI #AIAvatars
