SpaceX Targets Sept. 28 Starship Orbital Test

SpaceX, Starship, Starlink, orbital launch, September 28 2026, rockets, space technology · 2026-10-03

SpaceX is targeting September 28 for a Starship mission that could attempt to place the vehicle into orbit for the first time, according to TechCrunch’s September 15 report. The mission is also expected to test deployment of the first V3 Starlink satellites if the launch proceeds as planned. The date is a target rather than a guarantee, so weather, vehicle readiness and regulatory conditions could change the schedule. #SpaceX #Starship #Starlink
