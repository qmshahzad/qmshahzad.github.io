Meta Expands Its AI Glasses Line at Connect

Meta, Meta Connect 2026, Ray-Ban Meta, smart glasses, AI glasses, wearable AI, Muse · 2026-10-03

At Meta Connect, the company introduced Ray-Ban Meta Audio and expanded its AI glasses lineup, continuing its push to make wearable devices a primary interface for AI. Meta described the new Audio glasses as its first camera-free audio glasses and also announced broader styles and capabilities for its smart-glasses portfolio. The announcements show how Meta is extending AI beyond phones and screens into everyday wearable computing. #Meta #SmartGlasses #WearableAI
