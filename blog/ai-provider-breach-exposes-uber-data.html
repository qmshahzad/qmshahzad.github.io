AI Provider Breach Exposes Uber Data

Fireworks.ai, Uber, data breach, cybersecurity, third-party risk, AI infrastructure, data privacy · 2026-11-16

A reported breach at AI provider Fireworks.ai exposed driver’s-license information for at least 7,870 people in South Carolina, Massachusetts and Rhode Island. Attackers allegedly used stolen credentials to access Uber-related data, and Uber cut its connection to Fireworks. The incident shows how AI vendors can become a cybersecurity weak point. #Cybersecurity #DataBreach #AI
