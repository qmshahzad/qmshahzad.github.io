Apple’s New Mac mini and Mac Studio Are Now Available

Apple, Mac mini, Mac Studio, M6, M5 Ultra, Apple silicon, on-device AI, developers · 2026-10-03

Apple’s latest Mac mini and Mac Studio became available on September 22, with new M6, M5 Pro, M5 Max and M5 Ultra configurations aimed at demanding computing and on-device AI workloads. Apple highlights Neural Accelerators, large unified-memory options and Thunderbolt 5, with Mac Studio configurations reaching up to 512GB of unified memory. The new desktops are positioned for developers, creators and AI users who want substantial local compute. #Apple #MacStudio #OnDeviceAI
