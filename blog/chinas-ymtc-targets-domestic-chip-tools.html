China’s YMTC Targets Domestic Chip Tools

YMTC, NAND, flash memory, China, semiconductor equipment, data centers · 2026-11-11

YMTC, China’s largest flash-memory chipmaker, plans to source as much as 70% of the equipment for a planned fourth Wuhan factory from domestic suppliers, Reuters reports. The plant is expected to make advanced NAND chips for data centers and AI applications, with more than 300 stacked memory layers. #YMTC #NAND #Semiconductors
