Nvidia Faces Leadership Reset

Nvidia, Jensen Huang, Colette Kress, interim CEO, AI chips, semiconductors, AI industry · 2026-11-15

Nvidia co-founder Jensen Huang resigned as CEO and left the board after reports of extramarital affairs raised U.S. national-security concerns. CFO Colette Kress became interim CEO while Nvidia searches for a permanent successor. The leadership change lands at a sensitive moment for the company that supplies much of the computing power behind modern AI. #Nvidia #AI #Semiconductors
