FAA Expands AI Flight Routing

FAA, SMART, AI routing, aviation, air traffic, fuel efficiency, flight optimization · 2026-11-17

The FAA is expanding its SMART AI routing system nationwide after a Washington-area pilot program. The system reportedly rerouted 180 flights, saving an average of 10 minutes and nearly 80 gallons of fuel per flight. It is an early example of AI being used to optimize critical transportation infrastructure. #Aviation #AI #FAA
