OpenAI Closes $11 Billion Funding Round

OpenAI, funding, valuation, SoftBank, MGX, AI investment · 2026-11-08

OpenAI closed an initial $11 billion funding tranche at a $1.25 trillion valuation, according to Reuters. SoftBank and MGX participated. The round was originally targeting up to $30 billion, but the valuation is below the roughly $1.5 trillion level initially sought. #OpenAI #AIFunding #ArtificialIntelligence
