Stripe Pursues Acquisition of AI Gateway Startup OpenRouter

Stripe, OpenRouter, FinTech, Mergers and Acquisitions, AI Software · 2026-08-19

Stripe is moving beyond payment processing by pursuing the acquisition of OpenRouter. Integrating OpenRouter's smart AI routing network allows Stripe developers to dynamically select optimal LLMs, reducing latency and cost across automated transactional systems.
