100+ Companies Call for AI Cyber Defense

cybersecurity, AI cyberattacks, open letter, OpenAI, Anthropic, critical infrastructure · 2026-11-12

More than 100 companies, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, CrowdStrike and Fortinet, signed an open letter calling for stronger defenses against AI-powered cyber threats. They warn that advanced AI could make attacks more sophisticated and put hospitals, utilities and internet infrastructure at greater risk. #Cybersecurity #AISafety #TechPolicy
