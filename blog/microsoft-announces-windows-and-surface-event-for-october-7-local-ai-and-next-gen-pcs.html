Microsoft Announces Windows and Surface Event for October 7: Local AI and Next-Gen PCs

Microsoft event, Windows AI, Surface Laptop Ultra, RTX Spark, local AI, October 2026 · 2026-09-20

On September 15, 2026 Microsoft confirmed it will hold a dedicated Windows and Surface event in San Francisco on October 7. The stated theme is “how local AI will shape the next chapter of the PC.” Confirmed participants include Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella, Windows and Devices executive Pavan Davuluri, and Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang—signaling that hardware capable of running large models on-device will be a centerpiece. Industry attention is focused on the previously teased Surface Laptop Ultra, which pairs Nvidia’s RTX Spark platform with high unified memory and full CUDA support, enabling local inference of models with tens of billions of parameters. Broader Windows quality and AI feature updates are also expected. This will be Microsoft’s first major Windows-focused event in more than two years and arrives as the industry shifts from cloud-only AI experiences toward hybrid and fully local agents. For developers and IT teams the event should clarify the practical timeline for high-performance local AI hardware, developer tooling, and any new Windows APIs or Surface-specific capabilities. Whether you build native Windows applications, cross-platform mobile clients that talk to on-device models, or automation that bridges cloud and edge, the announcements will shape near-term architecture decisions.
