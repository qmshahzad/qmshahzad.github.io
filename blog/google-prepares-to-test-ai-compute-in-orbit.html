Google Prepares to Test AI Compute in Orbit

Google, Project Suncatcher, TPU, AI infrastructure, space computing, satellite, machine learning · 2026-10-01

Google’s Project Suncatcher is moving toward its first in-orbit test, with a prototype satellite intended to evaluate how Tensor Processing Units perform under radiation, vibration and thermal conditions. Google says the longer-term research question is whether space could support scalable machine-learning infrastructure using abundant sunlight and satellite-to-satellite links. The upcoming test is experimental, not a commercial space data center, but it marks an unusual direction for AI infrastructure research. #Google #AIInfrastructure #SpaceTech
