Meta Expands Muse With Real-Time Voice

Meta, Muse, realtime voice, AI avatar, conversational AI, assistant · 2026-11-13

Meta launched Muse Realtime Voice and Muse Realtime Avatar, allowing U.S. users to hold real-time voice conversations with its AI assistant. The avatar can display expressions and gestures synchronized with speech, while users can interrupt responses—another step toward more natural conversational AI. #Meta #Muse #AI
