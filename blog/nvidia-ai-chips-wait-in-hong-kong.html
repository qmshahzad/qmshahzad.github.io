Nvidia AI Chips Wait in Hong Kong

Nvidia, H200, Hong Kong, China, export controls, AI chips · 2026-11-10

Reuters reports that roughly 600,000 Nvidia advanced AI chips, including H200 processors, are stored in Hong Kong awaiting shipment to mainland China. Beijing has withheld approval for large-scale imports despite demand from Chinese technology firms, amid continuing U.S. export controls. #NVIDIA #AIChips #ExportControls
