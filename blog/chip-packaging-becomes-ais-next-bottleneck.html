Chip Packaging Becomes AI’s Next Bottleneck

advanced packaging, CoWoS, TSMC, Nvidia, AMD, semiconductors, AI chips · 2026-11-09

Reuters reports that AI demand has turned advanced chip packaging into a major bottleneck. TSMC dominates high-end CoWoS packaging, while Nvidia, AMD and suppliers are expanding capacity. Advanced-packaging revenue is forecast to surpass traditional packaging for the first time in 2026. #Semiconductors #AIChips #AdvancedPackaging
