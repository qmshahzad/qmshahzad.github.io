AI Selloff Pushes Nasdaq Into Correction Territory

Nasdaq, AI stocks, semiconductor selloff, Nvidia, Qualcomm, data centers, AI infrastructure, stock market · 2026-11-06

Technology stocks fell sharply as investors reassessed the pace and profitability of AI data-center spending. The Nasdaq entered correction territory, while semiconductor stocks slid after Qualcomm’s smartphone-chip sales missed estimates and concerns grew over potential scrutiny of data-center expansion. The move highlights how sensitive AI infrastructure valuations remain to spending, regulation and demand signals. #AI #StockMarket #Semiconductors
